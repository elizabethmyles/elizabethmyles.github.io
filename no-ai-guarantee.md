## My No A.I. Guarantee &#129302;

In case you're wondering, I, Elizabeth Myles, am a real person! To see photographic evidence, and to learn more about me, check out [my bio](https://elizabethmyles.com/about/) &#128522;.

I hereby guarantee that *for better or worse, every word of every book I have or will ever write and publish is 100% organically generated by my very own human brain.*

**I have not, do not, nor will I ever, use any type of Artificial Intelligence to generate story ideas, outlines, rough drafts, final drafts, revisions, re-writes, book descriptions, marketing materials, or any other aspect of my work.**

I may write my books slowly (too slowly, if you ask me) but I do write them all by myself, the hard way. Frankly, I would rather stop writing altogether than to do it otherwise. Because I don't only consider this to be "the hard way," but "the fun way." I actually enjoy writing, that's a big part of why I do it, and I cannot imagine delegating work that I care about so much to anyone (or anything) else.

Thank you for enabling me to live my dream, and for supporting hard-working indie authors and our books!

Sincerely,

Elizabeth Myles
